How it works
  • Poverty: everything that moves money to the people (taxes, public companies, conversions, kills) lifts people out of poverty, about 100M per $1T. Their rent pushes people back in. Each estimate is shown next to its button.
  • Goal: push the top 10's share of world wealth down to 12%. At 35% they own the world and you lose. Hold out 40 years for a draw.
  • Their money grows on its own: 5% a year on assets they own in countries, plus rent from the people. Owning housing doubles the rent. Cash parked offshore grows at only 2%.
  • Politics sets your costs. Left-leaning countries make taxing and nationalising cheaper, but the rich flee them sooner. Right-leaning countries cost more, and the rich lobby them more easily. Billionaires who own media push their country to the right every year. Poverty matters too: every year, countries where poverty has risen since the start drift right, and countries where it has fallen drift left. Campaign (1 action) shifts a country left. A public broadcaster (2 actions) stops their media pushing a country right for 5 years.
  • Wealth tax: each level takes 3% a year of their holdings in that country. At 9% (6% in left-leaning countries) they move their money offshore.
  • Make public (needs a centrist or left-leaning country; bigger sectors cost more, about 1.5 actions per $1T): seize every billionaire asset in one sector of one country. The backlash pushes the country a little to the right. Public companies pay 5% a year in profits to the people. The sector is safe for 3 years. After that they can lobby a right-leaning government to privatise it, and that rarely works. Everyone else moves 20% of their money in that country offshore.
  • Convert (2 actions): each religion makes them give in a different way. The odds depend on how widespread the religion is in their home country. Every failed attempt makes them more resistant.
  • Kill (4 actions, +1 per kill, once a year): half their fortune goes to the people. An heir inherits the rest and spends 3 years smearing you. Everyone else panics and moves 30% offshore.
  • Fight poverty (doesn't move the wealth share): rich, non-right-leaning countries can fund aid (1 action a level, up to 3) that sends 0.4% of their wealth a year to poorer countries. Any country that isn't far right can build free healthcare, education or public housing (3 actions each). Services are safe for 3 years, then right-leaning governments can be lobbied to privatise them. Right-leaning donors get lobbied to cut aid.
  • Actions: 6 a year, plus 1 for every 40M people your ongoing policies (taxes, public companies, conversions, aid, services) lift out of poverty that year (up to +5). One-off seizures and kills don't count. Unused actions carry over, up to 15.
  • Their turn: each billionaire gets 1 action, more as they get richer. They buy assets, lobby taxes down, push privatisation, fund the far right or flee offshore. A country that is 50% owned by them is lost.
