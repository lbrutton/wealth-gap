The Wealth Gap game (working title)

One line: Plague Inc in reverse, where the plague is the 100 richest people on earth.

You are the people of the world. On a world map, each turn you pick countries and other institutions to act through, and you use them to shrink inequality: raise taxes, nationalise industries, strengthen unions, sign treaties to close tax havens, slow down automation.

They are the 100 richest people on earth (parody versions), led by the top 10. After your turn, each of the top 10 takes theirs. They buy up a country's assets, lobby against your policies, fund far-right movements, stage false-flag attacks, bust unions, move their money offshore. You watch them undo your work.

The squeeze: their wealth grows by itself every turn, so if you do nothing you lose. They can't control governments directly, but every country has a capture meter that rises as they lobby and buy things up. Partly captured countries resist your policies. Fully captured ones are lost: you can no longer act through them.

Not just governments, there are other institutions you can act on in your turn: you can also act through religions (convert billionaires into "nice" ones who give their wealth away, start Jihads), or a secret cabal of rich-people hunters who make billionaires disappear and take their money (or they can do other things like trigger expensive divorces). The cabal can't be captured, but if it's exposed, you lose it.

The dark stuff: history says inequality only really drops after war, revolution, state collapse or plague. So those are on the menu too: start wars between countries, engineer a plague focused on working age men that makes labour scarce and pushes wages up, etc…

Win: the top 100's share of global wealth falls below a set threshold.
Lose: it rises above a higher one.

The amount of actions you can take in your turn is dependent on... something? Maybe the amount of people that were pulled out of poverty? I guess there's a base amount of actions, and then an action economy you play with to scale the amount of things you can do in your turn. Same goes for the billionaires
