# The Wealth Gap: economics model (v0.4)

## Two pots of money
- **The people:** one pot per country (P). The US starts at $28T, Nigeria at $1T. About $83T in total.
- **The top 10:** each billionaire's money sits in two places:
  - **Assets** in a country, split by sector (media, housing, energy, tech, retail). These earn well and give them power over that country.
  - **Offshore cash:** safe from you, but it earns less and gives no power.
- **The score** is the top 10's share of all money: rich / (rich + people). It starts at 15.5%. You win at 12% and lose at 35%.

The numbers are abstract. In reality the top 100 hold about 2% of global wealth. The game starts at 15.5% so the swings are visible.

## The engine: r > g
Each year, without anyone acting:
| Flow | Rate | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Returns on assets | 5% | Into their offshore cash |
| Returns on offshore cash | 2% | To them |
| Rent | 5% of the people's pot × how much of the country they own (housing counts double) | People → them |
| Growth of the people's economy | 2% | To the people |

They compound at 5% plus rent, and the people at 2%. Doing nothing raises their share by about 0.5 to 1 point a year, which is a loss in about 14 years.

## Capture: how money becomes power
**Capture** = their assets in a country / (their assets + the people's pot there).
- Higher capture means more rent and more expensive actions for you there.
- At 50% the country is **lost** and you can't act there any more.
- Buying assets moves their offshore cash into a country, raising capture there. Taxes push money back offshore, lowering it.

## Poverty is the exchange rate between money and actions
- Every $1T moved to the people lifts about **100M** people out of poverty. Indirect transfers count for less: kills at 40%, nationalising and public profits at 50%.
- Every $1T of rent they take pushes about **100M** people into poverty.
- Net people lifted in a year become bonus actions: **+1 per 15M**, up to +5, on top of 6 base. You can bank up to 15.

That closes your loop: moving money to the people earns you more actions, which move more money.

## Actions are your currency
Costs rise with capture and right-leaning politics:
| Action | Cost | What moves |
|---|---|---|
| Wealth tax level | 1 to 5 | 3% a year of their assets in that country → people, until they flee (at 9%, or 6% in left countries) |
| Make public | about 2 to 5, plus 1.5 per $1T seized | One sector's assets → people, plus 5% a year in public profits. Safe from privatisation for 3 years. Backlash: politics move right, and 20% of their money there goes offshore |
| Convert | 2 (odds based on how common the religion is in their home country) | Ongoing or one-off giving → people |
| Kill | 4, +1 per kill | Half the fortune → people. The heir smears you (−1 action a year for 3 years), and everyone else moves 30% offshore |
| Campaign | 1 | Politics move left |
| Public broadcaster | 2 | Stops their media moving politics right for 5 years |

## Politics: the multiplier
Each country has a lean from −1 (far left) to +1 (far right). It changes:
- your action costs (−1 to +2)
- whether you can make sectors public (only centrist or further left)
- how early they flee taxes (sooner in left countries)
- how often their lobbying succeeds (more often in right countries)

What moves it:
| Pushes left | Pushes right |
|---|---|
| Your campaigns: −0.08 | Their media: up to +0.05 a year |
| Falling poverty: −0.01 | Far-right funding: +0.07 |
| | Your nationalising backlash: +0.06 |
| | Rising poverty: +0.01 |

## Their side
- **Actions:** each billionaire gets 1 a year, +1 above $3T, +1 above $6T, and +1 while vengeful.
- **Priorities, in order:**
  1. Smear you, if they're a vengeful heir.
  2. Flee high taxes.
  3. Lobby a tax down.
  4. Push privatisation.
  5. Fund the far right.
  6. Buy assets with 60% of their cash.

## The loops
**Theirs (vicious):**
- Wealth buys assets, assets raise capture, capture raises rent, and rent adds wealth.
- More wealth gives them more actions.
- Owning media moves politics right, which makes your actions dearer and their lobbying easier.

**Yours (virtuous):**
- Transfers lift people, lifted people give you more actions, and more actions mean more transfers.
- Public companies compound for the people the way assets compound for them.
- Moving politics left makes everything cheaper, at the cost of quicker money flight.

**The pressure valve is offshore.** Every aggressive move pushes their money offshore. There it earns 2% instead of 5% and extracts no rent, so pushing money offshore is a partial win. But you can't touch it, and they spend it buying back into countries the moment the pressure drops.
